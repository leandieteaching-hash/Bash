# Incident Response Runbook

1. Confirm and classify the incident; appoint incident commander, communications lead and technical lead.
2. Preserve evidence: deployment digest, audit records, correlation IDs, logs, traces and database state.
3. Contain the issue by disabling affected integrations, revoking sessions, restricting traffic or rolling back.
4. Protect tenant confidentiality: do not place customer content or credentials in chat or public tickets.
5. Recover using verified deployment and data-recovery procedures.
6. Notify affected stakeholders according to contractual and legal obligations.
7. Complete a blameless post-incident review with timeline, root cause, control gaps and assigned actions.
